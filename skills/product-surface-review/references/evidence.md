# Evidence

Evidence shows a change the way its consumer meets it, captured from the running product in the
before and the after state.

1. Take the changes the user names, or every change in the review when they name none. Reuse the
   evidence the pull request already has, labelled with where it came from, and capture only what
   it is missing.
2. Run the product in each state, with the data, flags and accounts the change needs. Ask the user
   when the repository does not say how.
3. For each change, capture what its consumer sees in both states, following the same steps in each.
4. Put the evidence under the change in place of its `show-me` view. Keep that view beside it when
   the evidence alone is hard to comprehend, such as a full-page screenshot or a full JSON payload.
5. For a change you cannot show running, keep its `show-me` view and mark it as read in the code,
   not run.
6. Post the evidence where the review is read, and leave the environment as you found it.

Evidence is done when every change taken in step 1 has evidence from both states or a marked view.
