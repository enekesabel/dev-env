# Consumers and surfaces

Start from the repositories the user names, or the current repository when they name none.
When the product already has a `PRODUCT_SURFACES.md`, start from it: check it against the
repositories and propose what to add, remove or change.

1. List the consumers. For each, say who they are and the goal they come with.
2. List the surfaces, numbered. For each, say what it is, which consumers meet it, and where in the
   repositories it lives.
3. Write down what you are unsure of as questions for the user: a consumer you inferred, a surface
   you could not open, a split the user may see differently.

The list is done when every consumer meets at least one surface, every surface names at least one
consumer, and every place you found where a consumer meets the product belongs to a surface.

## Shape

```markdown
### Consumers
- **<consumer>**: <who they are>. Goal: <what they come to the product for>.

### Surfaces
1. **<surface>**: <what it is>.
   - Consumers: <consumer>, <consumer>
   - Lives in: <paths, per repository>

### Questions
- <what you are unsure of, and what you need from the user>
```
